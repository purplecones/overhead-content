# OH-01 Peregrine

The app's original concept airliner, and the model every aircraft wears until a more specific one matches.
It is original work by the Overhead project: generated procedurally by the project's own aircraft tooling and exported to GLB with Y up and the nose along -Z, 23.05 m long with a 25.3 m span.
